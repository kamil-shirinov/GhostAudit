## Known limitations

- **The sample scores are optimistic.** All 32 labelled samples were used to design and tune the rules, including a few category hints, so they are a development set, not a test set. The custom cases and label-free checks above are our evidence beyond them.
- **Some resources can only be linked by inference.** Primary accounts with no identifier link are matched to the deletion worker's activity; on the samples this was right 11 times and wrong 3. Anonymised analytics records with no identifiers are not linked at all, and this causes most of the remaining recall gap.
- **The multimodal cases are unlabelled.** About 11 evaluation cases rest on a screenshot or document. We checked the extracted IDs against the screenshots by eye, but no gold answer covers these cases.
- **Catalog links are trusted.** In case_63 the data catalog links a marketing profile to the requester, but the screenshot shows another person's name and email. We report the record and flag the mismatch rather than guess.
- **A missing retained record is not flagged.** Policy requires retained records to stay available, but no allowed operation restores one. When a snapshot omits a retained audit record, we still verify it as present, as the gold answers do. Skipping such records lowered exact sequences on the samples from 17 to 14, so we kept this behaviour. As a result, one evaluation case (case_46) cites a snapshot that omits the record.
- **Evidence from before the request is ignored for state.** Such evidence belongs to an earlier lifecycle, as with case_46's March deletion, but the rule drops a verification step in nine evaluation cases whose only evidence is older than the request.
- **Tombstone redaction relies on known field names.** A tombstone is judged redacted when it shows no name, email, phone or hash under the usual field names. A name printed under an unexpected label would be missed; an explicit allowlist of tombstone fields would close this, but it changes nothing on the current data.
- **No admissible evidence means no provable verdict.** When nothing about a person is admissible at audit_at, the agent returns COMPLETE with no steps, because the output contract has no "unknown" status. Custom case C3 shows this deliberately; no evaluation case reaches it.
